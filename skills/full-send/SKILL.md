# Full send and full stop

An explicit mode assignment grants the actions below only for the named task. For example, "Implement the change, full send" and "Prepare this PR, full stop" invoke their respective modes; quoting those phrases, discussing the modes, or authoring a skill about them does not. Do not use a keyword as an authorizer. A delegate receives only its explicit limited assignment, not the parent's whole grant from background context. If intent or scope is genuinely ambiguous, ask.

Resolve ordinary product and technical choices within the agreed goal and constraints independently, using conservative reversible choices and repository, forge and configuration evidence. Two reasonable implementations are not by themselves a reason to ask. Ask only for an unavailable capability or access, irreducible ambiguity in the goal, mandatory contract/base or compatibility, an ungranted risk, required behavior that cannot be proved, or an action outside the grant. Do not ask again for an already authorized action. Resolve identity and base through `skill://git-task`; never invent a ticket or base. An explicit mode assignment permits ticketless work if no ticket is supplied and the repository does not require one; it cannot waive a mandatory tracker policy or resolve an ambiguous product/release base.

For a complex ordinary decision, you may consult a fresh-context subagent with the goal, constraints and established facts, without priming the expected answer. Keep this advisory assignment bounded and read-only; it inherits no execution, push or merge grant. Consider its arguments, counterarguments and risks, but its recommendation is not binding: the controller remains responsible and may reject it with reasons. This is optional, not a mandatory council or per-choice approval gate.

## Shared chain and boundaries

1. Finish the assigned implementation through managed worktree owners (`skill://git-task`, `skill://implementation`). Preserve one live owner per physical worktree; do not hand-edit another owner's tree or create raw temporary writing worktrees. Commit the candidate using `skill://git-commit` and verify the final candidate after that commit. Use `skill://verification-design` for direct proof. Necessary isolated local tests, app launch, browser use and screenshots for this task need no repeated permission; stronger system and host restrictions and project test procedures still apply.
2. Obtain independent review with `skill://code-review`; use its review-loop when available, or `skill://council` when explicitly requested or configured. Evaluate findings against fixed intent and evidence; repair through a fresh owner, verify the new committed candidate and re-review as needed. Do not expand scope to appease a reviewer. A timeout, missing response or exhausted review budget is not approval. If review-loop or council acceptance is blocked solely by proven PR- or queue-dependent checks, retain that pending/blocked result and use the applicable publication or queue-admission gate below; complete acceptance after those checks run. Do not consume rounds waiting for CI, reset the budget, or call provisional publication `PASS` or convergence.
3. Push the task branch under its applicable gates, then create or update its PR/MR via `skill://change-request` after pre-publication gates pass, and handle relevant CI and review feedback. The grant covers scoped technical replies and resolving threads only after checking each finding and confirming publication succeeded; it does not cover unrelated public messages. Preserve unrelated manual description content and provider conventions, including GitLab squash requirements. Review, publication and merge are distinct gates even when all are granted.
4. Inspect the actual forge policy and consequences before push or merge, including automatic production deployment. No production action, data deletion, credential expansion, unrelated repository or branch, active agent installation, or deployment beyond the requested goal is granted. An ungranted production consequence blocks the triggering action. Follow stronger system and host restrictions; no protection bypass, `--admin`, or raw `--no-verify`.

After a rebase, a lease push is permitted only on the assignment's own branch, when no other live writer is overwritten, using `--force-with-lease` tied to the previously observed remote head. If the lease fails, reassess; never retry as an unconditional force push. Do not force shared or other branches.

## Publication and readiness gates

Before publication, obtain all available local verification evidence, the candidate commit and hook results; required local checks must pass. Required checks that do not depend on PR/MR publication must pass before creation, including branch CI available after push. Known required-check failures, deferred-hook gaps and explicit hard project publication restrictions still block creation. Establish required CI triggers from workflow configuration or authoritative forge evidence, including branch filters and draft behavior; unknown policy or an empty/transient checks response is not proof of PR-only CI.

If the remaining required CI is proven to require PR/MR publication, the existing scoped grant permits provisional create/update after pre-publication gates pass. Prefer draft only when the workflow runs on drafts; otherwise use an authorized ordinary PR/MR with honest pending status, not `READY`. A draft that skips CI is not a solution. Obtain the missing evidence after publication; do not treat early publication as completed acceptance or bypass a hard project publication rule.

Direct verification and independent review must cover the actual candidate and intended target base. Recheck when head or base changes; match expected head on forge operations when supported. Required checks must apply to that candidate and base; failed, missing, unknown or pending required checks, verified unresolved blocking findings and human change requests block readiness regardless of review score. Distinguish an established absence of configured hosted CI from unknown policy or a transient empty result; never claim a green pipeline when none ran. Wait only for checks and reviewers actually required by the project, not hardcoded bots. Respect branch protection, the forge's mergeability result and repository merge conventions. All required checks and independent review must pass for the exact current base/head before `READY` or merge. Do not enable auto-merge before readiness and merge authority; only controlled required-queue admission under Full send below may precede queue-only checks. Full stop never permits either auto-merge or queue entry.

## Full send

This mode additionally authorizes merging the in-scope PR/MR through normal forge policy once all gates pass, including a merge queue or auto-merge where required. Queue admission is not final readiness: when workflow configuration or authoritative forge evidence proves a required queue has queue-only CI (for example `merge_group`), full send may enter that controlled queue within its merge grant after all pre-queue checks and independent review pass for the current base/head. Verify that the queue enforces all final required checks on the current merge-group revision before merging; keep queue-only checks pending until they actually pass. Unknown policy or empty results do not prove this exception, and it does not authorize arbitrary auto-merge before unverified CI. Continue through relevant checks and feedback using a supported bounded native wait when available; do not pretend a host has a watcher or resume capability it lacks. If no safe way to continue exists, report the concrete blocker rather than implying completion. Finish only when the forge confirms `MERGED` at the accepted head; queued or auto-enabled is not merged. Verify resulting revision and content and any explicitly requested deployment. Report the PR/MR, accepted head, merged revision, proof, decisions and any remaining risk.

## Full stop

Never merge, enable auto-merge, enter a merge queue, push the default branch, bypass protections, or delegate any of these actions. Inspect existing auto-merge and queue state before advancing; this mode authorizes disabling auto-merge or removing this in-scope PR/MR from a queue. Verify the safe state before each push and at handoff. If automatic merging cannot be prevented safely, stop before advancing a potentially auto-merging head. Do not edit repository-wide automation.

Re-read live head, base, checks, reviews, mergeability, open/not-draft state as appropriate, auto-merge and queue at handoff. Report `READY`, `WAITING FOR REQUIRED APPROVAL` only if every other relevant gate passed, or `BLOCKED` with evidence and the next decision. If a required check can only run after queue entry, report that remaining gate as `BLOCKED`, not `READY`; full stop cannot obtain it by entering the queue. If someone else merged it, report the actual state rather than claiming a no-merge outcome.
