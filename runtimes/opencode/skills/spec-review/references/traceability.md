# Traceability

Trace material intent through the spec:

`requirement -> decision or behavior -> acceptance criterion -> verification evidence`

Look for:

- requirements with no implementation consequence;
- implementation commitments with no supporting requirement or decision;
- acceptance criteria that do not prove the requirement they claim to cover;
- material behavior with no plausible verification;
- conflicting requirements, decisions, or acceptance criteria.

Prefer a direct explanation of gaps over forcing a traceability matrix when the artifact is simple enough to inspect directly.
